## Clash of Clans names and identifiers

Entity names and numeric export IDs in `GameCatalogue.kt` describe Clash of Clans game content and remain associated with Supercell. The mapping was cross-checked against the [community village export mapping by rahulkhatri137](https://gist.github.com/rahulkhatri137/a8449943df45100c5f1e1359cd9ec67a) and [cerealkillahh's building ID table](https://github.com/cerealkillahh/clash-of-clans-data/blob/main/data/buildings.json). The gist does not state a license; COCwidgt does not bundle or redistribute either source file, code, or artwork. The catalogue is a small list of names/IDs maintained in this app. No third-party game artwork is included.
